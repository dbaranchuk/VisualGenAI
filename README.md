# Diffusion Models: Course and Seminars

Course materials and research seminars on diffusion models and related generative
methods, including image, video, 3D, and language generation.

## Visual GenAI course

The **Spring 2026 Visual GenAI course** covers diffusion fundamentals, flow matching,
efficient sampling, few-step generation, visual autoregression, and video and 3D
models. It includes 12 syllabus topics, seven assignments, and exam preparation
materials. Slides are in English and recordings are in Russian.

**[Open the course README →](visual_genai_course_2026/README.md)** for the syllabus, slides, recordings,
assignments, exam information, and course contacts.

## Research Seminars on Diffusion Models

The seminar series explores current research in diffusion models and related
methods. The archive contains 151 entries from 2023 to September 2026, with an
English index, paper references, and links to recordings in Russian.

**[Open the research seminar README →](research_seminars/README.md)** to browse the
archive, find slides, and join the Telegram group.
